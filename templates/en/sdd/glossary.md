# Glossary

<!-- sdd-template: 1 -->

The words this project uses with a meaning of its own. One row per term, added the first
time the term shows up in a conversation, a spec or an ADR — never invented in advance.
A term belongs here when two people (or a person and an agent) could reasonably read it
differently: domain nouns ("order", "tenant", "session"), states ("archived") and any
word the user gave a narrower meaning than the dictionary one.

The `Source` column is what keeps this honest: where the meaning was fixed — the person
who said it, the spec, or the ADR that decided it. A row without a source is a guess.
